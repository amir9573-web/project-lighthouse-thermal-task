A thermal engineering team is reviewing a pulsed heater assembly before approving a longer duty cycle. Its core and enclosure warm at different rates, cooling changes with fan state, and the heater command may precede delivered heat. The measurement campaign includes sensor calibration records, four identification runs, two separate validation runs, and a proposed operating cycle.

Use the records in /app/data to establish the assembly's thermal parameters and assess the proposed cycle. The physical model, acquisition conventions, admissible parameter ranges, uncertainty definition, and output contract are recorded in /app/data/measurement-specification.md and /app/data/protocol.json. Raw measurements include unavailable readings and acquisition flags. The validation runs are reserved for evaluating predictions rather than estimating the model.

Deliver /app/output/result.json with the sensor calibrations, physical parameter estimates, heater delay, delay comparison, local parameter covariance, leave-one-run-out estimates, and validation errors. Include the proposed cycle's nominal and ensemble core peaks and the largest permitted common multiplier of its heater power under the specified operating envelope. Deliver /app/output/forecast.csv with the complete nominal and ensemble temperature histories at that multiplier. Both files must use the quantities, ordering, and units defined in the output contract. The engineering decision depends on the transient core temperature throughout the cycle, including cooling periods after a heater command ends.
